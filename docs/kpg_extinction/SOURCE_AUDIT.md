# Source Audit — K–Pg Extinction WikiGraph v0.5

This is a first-pass source audit for a public demo. It is not a full systematic review.

## Source-weighting principles

1. **Secondary reviews carry article-weighting authority.** Schulte et al. 2010 is treated as a high-value synthesis node.
2. **Primary geochronology is powerful but narrow.** Schoene 2019 and Sprain 2019 are strong for timing but cannot alone settle broad causality.
3. **Modeling papers are evidence nodes, not final verdicts.** Chiarenza 2020 and Hull 2020 support impact-primary interpretations but remain dependent on model choices and input assumptions.
4. **Fossil-diversity claims must show sampling-bias caveats.** Condamine 2021 and related recent work are connected through a sampling-bias guardrail node.
5. **Wikipedia policy nodes are not scientific evidence.** They govern how source-backed views should be represented to editors.

## High-risk claims deliberately parked

- `claim:deccan_sole_established` — shows how a minority/overclaim version is retained but visibly weakened.
- `claim:impact_only_no_background` — shows that even majority-side shorthand can be overclean and needs caveats.
- `evidence:popular_meteor_shortcut` — M-only narrative shortcut; not scientific evidence.

## Publication caution

Before using this as a public pitch, do a second-pass literature check for all recent 2025 nodes and decide whether they belong in the article-facing view or only in an audit/monitoring view.
