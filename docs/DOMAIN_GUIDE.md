# Domain Guide

## Common node types

All domains use the same basic node types, but each domain has different evidence standards.

## Domain-specific examples

### History / humanities

Strong evidence may include primary texts, securely dated artifacts, ancient DNA, stratigraphy, or philology. Mythic parallels and narrative analogies must be M-tagged unless tied to independent evidence.

Main demo: Jomon-associated paternal lineage persistence, Dotaku ritual discontinuity, and Kiki mythic structures as a scoped abductive hypothesis. D-M55 frequency alone must not prove conquest; Kiki myth must not be direct event record; Dotaku discontinuity must not be single-cause proof.

### Biomedicine

Separate in vitro, animal model, observational human data, randomized controlled trials, and meta-analysis. Never upgrade animal-model evidence to human clinical efficacy without explicit risk tagging.

### Social science

Separate correlation, natural experiment, quasi-experimental design, randomized intervention, and mechanism. Causal claims require design metadata and confounder discussion.

### Climate / earth science

Separate observation, model ensemble, attribution study, paleoclimate proxy, and single-event evidence. Single events can motivate hypotheses but do not prove long-term trends by themselves.

Domain id: `climate_earth`.

### AI / computer science

Separate benchmark result, implementation detail, reproducibility evidence, external evaluation, and real-world deployment. Benchmark performance is not equivalent to general capability or safety.

### Law / policy / ethics

Separate legal interpretation, precedent, normative claim, empirical policy impact, and ethical justification. A policy being lawful or desirable is not the same as it being empirically effective.
